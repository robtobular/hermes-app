Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

Hermes Personal Assistant ("the App") is a single-user, personal productivity tool built exclusively for Rob Wiens. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when connecting to Google Workspace APIs (Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Contacts).

2. Data Usage & Collection

The App accesses Google user data solely to provide personal assistant features requested by the account owner, including:

  • Reading Google Calendar events to compile daily schedule briefings.
  • Drafting calendar events and email responses for explicit user review and approval.
  • Checking recent emails for appointment and flight confirmations.
  • Saving subscription summaries to Google Sheets.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

No Data Is Sold or Shared: User data accessed via Google Workspace APIs is strictly used by the App for the user's explicit assistant requests. No user data is transferred, sold, or shared with third parties, advertisers, or external data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time by the user via Google Account Security Settings.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact Rob Wiens at rob2600@gmail.com.
